OUR MISSION

Music education should not depend on what a family can afford.

Across communities, instruments sit unused in closets, attics, rehearsal rooms, and storage spaces. At the same time, students can miss the opportunity to join band, orchestra, or music programs simply because they do not have access to an instrument.

Note by Note connects those two realities. We collect used instruments, help prepare them for a new musician, and match them with students and schools where they can keep making music.

01

Keep instruments playing

Instead of collecting dust, used instruments can return to classrooms and rehearsal rooms.

02

Expand access

We help reduce one of the biggest barriers to participation in school music: having an instrument.

03

Build community

Donors, repair partners, educators, and students come together around a shared love of music.

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

I know what one instrument can mean.

Note by Note began with a simple belief: a love of music should never be limited by access to an instrument.

As a musician, I have seen how an instrument can become more than an object. It can be a way to find confidence, discipline, friendship, expression, and a place to belong. I started Note by Note to help instruments that are no longer being played find their way to students who are ready to make them sing again.
