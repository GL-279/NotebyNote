OUR FOUNDER

Meet Jitong Lei.

Note by Note began with a simple belief: a love of music should never be limited by access to an instrument.

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

I know what one instrument can mean.

As a musician, I have seen how an instrument can become more than an object. It can be a way to find confidence, discipline, friendship, expression, and a place to belong.

I started Note by Note to help instruments that are no longer being played find their way to students who are ready to make them sing again.

— Jitong Lei, Founder

Giving used instruments a second life—and students a first note.
