OUR MISSION

Music education should not depend on what a family can afford.

Note by Note turns unused instruments into opportunities for students.

WHY NOTE BY NOTE

There is already music waiting to be passed on.

In the music nonprofit world, few organizations are led by students themselves. As part of a new generation of musicians, we want to help bridge generations—carrying forward the stories and legacies of the musicians who came before us while creating new opportunities for young learners today.

We see ourselves as both storytellers and guardians of the instruments entrusted to us. Every instrument has a history, and instead of allowing that story to end in a closet or storage room, we help it continue in the hands of a student who can write its next chapter.

Note by Note connects those two realities. We collect used instruments, help prepare them for a new musician, and match them with students and schools where they can keep making music—passing not only an instrument from one musician to another, but also its story, possibility, and legacy.
